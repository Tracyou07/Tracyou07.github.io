3D Reconstruction & World-Model Prediction
  • Investigating single-view and multi-view 3D reconstruction pipelines, benchmarking depth-based (DPT) and correspondence-based (DUST3R, CUT3R, Human3R) methods for consistent geometry from monocular images.
  • Reproducing modern 3D point correspondence methods to construct dense and temporally stable point clouds for downstream world-model prediction.
  • Building a world-model-style latent dynamics module to maintain temporal consistency across frames and reduce artifacts (mesh-body interpenetration, foot-sliding, geometry drift).
  • Exploring geometry-aware loss functions (Chamfer distance, normal consistency, contact constraints) to enforce physical plausibility in human–environment interactions.

Research 1: Training-Free Decoding for Hallucination Mitigation in LVLMs
  • Systematically analyzed mainstream decoding strategies (DoLa, VCD, DAMO, SPIN) on LLaVA, mPLUG-Owl2, InstructBLIP.
  • MAP: Proposed a 2D semantic-map interpretation of hidden states, introducing Layer-Wise Criss-Cross Attention and Global–Local Logit Fusion to gather factual cues across layers.
  • Achieved state-of-the-art hallucination suppression on MME, POPE, and MMHal-Bench across multiple 7B LVLMs.
Research 2: Token Efficiency & Visual Token Engineering for LVLM Acceleration
  • STAR: Dual-phase token reduction — early-stage pruning via visual self-attention, late-stage pruning via cross-modal attention. Achieved 95% token reduction and 58% FLOPs reduction while maintaining 101.3% performance.
  • Integrated STAR into LLaVA-1.5, LLaVA-NeXT, mPLUG-Owl2, and Qwen-VL; outperformed FastV, FasterVLM, and SparseVLM baselines.
  • SeeMe: First training-free hallucination mitigation framework via visual token engineering — Cross-Modal Attention Selection → Semantic Token Merging → Attention-Based Refinement.
